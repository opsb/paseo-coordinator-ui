Coordinator shows the cards that [paseo-coordinator](https://github.com/opsb/paseo-coordinator) has waiting for you, one at a time, with its replies as buttons. A Coordinator row in the sidebar carries the number of cards waiting; pressing it opens the deck.

Each card is a slide: the stream's motif and name, the pull requests and tickets it is working on as links, your own last words on it, what changed since, the ask, the coordinator's recommendation and evidence, and any project rule that applies. A Decide card offers Yes and No, a Do card Done, an Accept card Accept and Not yet. Every card also offers Park, Skip, Remind at a time you type, and a line for a reply in your own words. Refresher and More send that word and open the coordinator's chat, where it answers; a Chat button in the header opens the same chat for anything longer.

How it works: the plugin's daemon side reads the inbox the coordinator's worker keeps in `~/.local/state/coordinator/<scope>/inbox.md`, with the sweep worker's report behind each card, and watches the files, so a card appears as soon as the worker adds it and leaves as soon as the coordinator ticks it off. A reply is sent to the coordinator session (the live session labelled `coord-role=coordinator`) as a chat message that names the card, and the coordinator relays it and closes the card exactly as if you had typed the word in its chat. The deck moves to the next card at once.

Setup: install paseo-coordinator on the machine that runs the Paseo daemon and start a coordinator with `/coordinator` in a session there. Cards are shown in the order the worker added them; the coordinator's own ranking applies in its chat. A `nudge` card is the coordinator's to act on and is not shown. Set `COORD_STATE_ROOT` in the daemon's environment if the coordinator keeps its state somewhere other than the default. Nothing is sent anywhere but your own Paseo daemon.
